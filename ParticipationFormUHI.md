Exploring comfortable and effective User Interfaces in Health Applications

Invitation and brief summary

This is an invitation to participate in a focus group aimed at garnering user feedback into the availability, useability and comfort of use of potential user interfaces (UI) for the MauiScreenTime screen tracker app.

Participants will be invited to review a mock up UI for the software, given tasks to navigate it and asked questions about their experience culminating in a group discussion that will be recorded.

This is to gather data on the user experience (UX) in order to improve upon the mock up design and to inform further application development.


What are the possible benefits of taking part?

Contributing to the useability and availability of an emerging digital health application. The opportunity to explore and discuss what might improve the participants experience of using the application.

Data gathered will not be sensitive, will be anonymised and carries no risk.

The study should be incredibly simple and direct, using open ended questions to gather user sentiment and collate the data once anonymised to find repeating trends within all contributions.

Participants are welcome to withdraw consent at any time, contacting 19015831@uhi.ac.uk

Information will be anonymised and the recording will be destroyed per UHI's own data retention scheme.

The results will be condensed into actionable tasks and used to inform development of the UI and application functionality.

The study will primarily involve UHI students of varying ages. It will be reviewed by the MauiScreenTime development team.

Further contact details are Lauren Keenan 19015831@uhi.ac.uk

Participants will be invited to a Microsoft teams meeting once enough have consented via the consent form.
