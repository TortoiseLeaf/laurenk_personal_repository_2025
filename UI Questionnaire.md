**MAUIScreenTime – UI usability questionnaire**

Open ended questions to collect qualitative feedback on the usability of the UI.

What do I want from users feedback:

1. Opening and warm-up questions: First impressions, gauging the obviousness of the app’s purpose.
    
2. Usability and Navigation: Is the UI clear to use, and easy to navigate?
    
3. Design and Emotional response: Is the UI something you want to use or are comfortable looking at/navigating?
    
4. Features and Functionality: Do the features make sense and is the functionality understandable
    
5. Closing strategy questions: What would make you keep using or stop using this UI? 
    


**Opening & Warm-up Questions**

- What is your first impression of this interface?

- What do you think this product does, just by looking at it?

- How does this interface compare to similar apps you might use for this purpose?


**Usability & Navigation Questions**

- Can you tell me where you would look for the CO2e of your screen time?

- How easy or difficult is it to navigate this screen?

- What is the most confusing part of this interface?


**Visual Design & Emotional Response Questions**

- How do you feel about the colours and layout used here?

- Does this design look trustworthy and professional to you?

- Does anything make you feel frustrated while looking at this, if so, what?


**Feature & Functionality Questions**

- If you could change one thing about this interface, what would it be?

- Which feature here do you think is the most useful, and which is the least useful?

- What do you think is missing from this interface?


**Closing & Strategy Questions**

- What would make you stop using this application?

- Based on what you've seen, what would make you want to use this product daily?




Thanks for your time and participation
